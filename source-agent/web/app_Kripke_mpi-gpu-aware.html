Kripke Sn transport, GPU-aware MPI sweep
Distinct build (ENABLE_GPU_AWARE_MPI+CHAI): device pointers passed straight to MPI_Isend/Irecv for the neighbor plane exchange, with a device sync before send. Wants co-located GPUs on a fast link. Backend may be CUDA or HIP; kind recorded as cuda but applies to rocm builds too.
srun kripke --arch CUDA --procs npx,npy,npz  # built with ENABLE_GPU_AWARE_MPI+ENABLE_CHAI
