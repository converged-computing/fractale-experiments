Reading this

Each application was submitted twice per replicate: once with no requirements stated (base) and once describing what it needs (subsystem). Placement is measured before dispatch, so it holds whether or not the job then ran. Runtimes come from the flux job eventlog rather than the harness's poll, and every figure of merit is parsed from the application's own output. Ratios are subsystem divided by base, and whether that is an improvement depends on the metric, which is why the direction is stated everywhere.
